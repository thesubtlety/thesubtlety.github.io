I’m Noah Potti, a security engineer with a background in red teaming, adversarial engineering, and cyber defense. I co-founded Adversis, where I work with companies to assess risk, test systems, and improve the security and privacy of their products.

I’m interested in how systems fail, what attackers can actually do (and not do), and which changes make a meaningful difference. Finding a security problem is often easier than getting it fixed. I enjoy working with the people who build and operate systems to do both.

Much of my research involves identity, credentials, cloud and SaaS systems, post-exploitation activity, and the effectiveness of security controls. I sometimes write about these topics and build tools to explore them.
